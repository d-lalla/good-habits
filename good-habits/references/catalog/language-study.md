# Language study

Spend some minutes each day on a foreign language.

## Why it helps
Languages reward frequency more than long sessions: ten minutes a day keep words fresh better than an hour
once a week. Any method counts: an app, a course, a podcast, a conversation.

## Start small
- Ten minutes at a fixed moment. Put the language name in the habit `title` (e.g. "Spanish").
- Mix methods to keep it pleasant.

## How to log
- Value: minutes. Quick amounts: 10, 15. Several logs per day add up.

## Messages
Fill the numbers from `today`.
- start: "{target} minutes of language practice is today's example."
- midway: "{value} of {target} minutes so far today."
- almost there: "{remaining} minutes to go for today's {target}."
