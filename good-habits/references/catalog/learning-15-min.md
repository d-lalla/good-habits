# Learning 15 minutes

A short daily block to learn something chosen by the user: a skill, a course, a topic.

## Why it helps
A fixed short block makes learning a routine instead of a plan for "when there is time". Fifteen minutes
is an example: small enough to fit, long enough to make progress.

## Start small
- Agree on one subject at a time and put it in the habit `title`.
- Same moment each day; have the material ready before.

## How to log
- Value: minutes. Quick amount: 15. Several logs per day add up.

## Messages
Fill the numbers from `today`.
- start: "{target} minutes of learning is today's example."
- midway: "{value} of {target} minutes so far today."
- almost there: "{remaining} minutes to go for today's {target}."
