# Meditation

A few minutes of sitting quietly with attention on the breath or the present moment.

## Why it helps
Many people use meditation to pause and notice how they are. A few minutes a day are enough to make it a
habit. It is not a treatment: anyone dealing with anxiety, low mood or past trauma talks with a
professional, and stops if a practice makes them feel worse.

## Start small
- Start with one to five minutes at a fixed moment (after waking, before bed).
- Any method is fine: an app, a timer, counting breaths. The skill does not teach one.

## How to log
- Value: minutes. Quick amounts: 5, 10. Several sessions per day add up.

## Messages

- start: "{target} minutes of meditation is today's example."
