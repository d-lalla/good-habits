# Reach out

Get in touch with someone the user cares about: a message, a call, a visit.

## Why it helps
Relationships fade when contact only happens by chance. A small weekly intention keeps friends and family
close without needing big plans.

## Start small
- One or two contacts a week. A short message counts.
- Think of someone not heard from in a while.

## How to log
- One log per contact. No value.
- **Never record who it was** or what was said. If the user mentions a name, do not store it.

## Messages
Fill the numbers from `today`. A quiet week is just a quiet week.
- start: "New week: {target} contacts with people you care about is the plan."
- midway: "{value} of {target} contacts this week."
- almost there: "{remaining} more and this week's {target} contacts are done."
