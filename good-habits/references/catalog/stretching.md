# Stretching

A few minutes of gentle stretching each day.

## Why it helps
Short, gentle stretching can make the body feel less stiff, especially after sitting or on waking. A few
minutes done often matter more than long sessions. Stretching should never hurt; anyone with pain or an
injury asks a professional which movements suit them. The skill does not prescribe exercises.

## Start small
- Two to five minutes, at a fixed moment: after getting up, after work, before bed.
- Simple movements the user already knows are enough.

## How to log
- Value: minutes. Quick amounts: 5, 10. Several logs per day add up.
