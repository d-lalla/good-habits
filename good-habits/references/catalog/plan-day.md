# Plan the day

Take a few minutes to decide the day's priorities before starting.

## Why it helps
Choosing the two or three things that matter before the day fills up makes it easier to do them. It takes
five minutes and needs no special tool.

## Start small
- Write the top three tasks on paper or in the user's own tool. Nothing is stored here.
- Same moment each workday: with the first coffee, before opening email.

## How to log
- One log when the plan is made. No value. The plan's content is never recorded.

## Messages
Fill the numbers from `today`. Leave out the numbers while `week.scheduled` is 0.
- before: "A few minutes to plan the day, if it helps."
- after: "Day planned. This week {week.done} of {week.scheduled} workdays."
