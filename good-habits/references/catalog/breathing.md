# Breathing pauses

A minute or two of slow, calm breathing, a few times a day.

## Why it helps
Pausing to breathe slowly is something that can be done anywhere, and many people use it to reset between
tasks. Keep it gentle: anyone who feels dizzy stops and breathes normally. It is not a treatment for any
condition; breathing problems or panic attacks are for a professional.

## Start small
- One or two pauses a day, tied to moments that exist: before opening email, after a meeting.
- One minute is a session. No special technique required: slow, comfortable breaths.

## How to log
- One log per session. Optional value: minutes.

## Messages
Fill the numbers from `today`.
- start: "{target} breathing pauses is today's example."
- midway: "{value} of {target} pauses so far today."
- almost there: "{remaining} more pause for today's {target}."
