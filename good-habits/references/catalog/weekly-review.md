# Weekly review

Once a week, look back at the past week and ahead at the next one.

## Why it helps
A short weekly look at what happened and what is coming keeps loose ends from piling up and makes the
next week feel planned rather than rushed.

## Start small
- 15-30 minutes, same day each week. Three questions are enough: what got done, what is open, what matters
  next week.
- The notes stay in the user's own tool; nothing is stored here.

## How to log
- One log per week when the review is done. No value. Any day Monday to Sunday counts for that week.

## Messages
Fill the values from `today`.
- before: "Weekly review this week, around {time} on the planned day."
- after: "Weekly review done for this week."
