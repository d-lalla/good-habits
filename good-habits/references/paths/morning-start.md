# Morning start

A short morning sequence: water, a few minutes of movement, a plan for the day.

## Steps
| Offset (min) | Habit | Overrides |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | `hydration` | — |
| 10 | `stretching` | — |
| 30 | `plan-day` | — |

## Notes
- The anchor is the time the user usually gets up. 07:00 is an example.
- `hydration` is the whole day's water; the time is only the cue for the first glass.
- `plan-day` keeps its workdays; on free days the user can skip it or change its days.
