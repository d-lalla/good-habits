Habits that last, without streak anxiety.

Good Habits turns your AI assistant into a calm habit coach. Pick a few habits, start small, say what you did in plain words, and see how it's going: "this week 4 of 7". No points, no badges, no guilt.

Install View on GitHub
$npx skills add d-lalla/good-habits -g
Why

Kind to you, exact about numbers

Most trackers punish a missed day, and guilt is what makes people quit. Good Habits is built the other way round.

Describes, never judges

"4 sessions so far", never "you failed". No streak to defend.

Rest is not failure

Planned skips and pauses are left out of every count.

Small steps

Every default is an example. Changes are proposals you can decline.

Your data stays yours

Two plain files in a folder you choose. Numbers come from a script, not guesses.

Just talk

No commands to learn

Say what you did. The assistant logs it and tells you where you are, in your language.

YouTwo glasses and a 500 ml bottle today. I'm skipping Sunday, I'll be travelling.
AssistantLogged: 1000 of 1500 ml so far today. Sunday is marked as a rest day for water.
YouGoing to bed now.
AssistantIn bed at 22:50. Last 7 days: 5 of 6 evenings by 23:00.
A week

Progress you can read at a glance

On Sunday, if you like, a short summary: what went well, what changed, at most one suggestion. Missed days stay open, rest days don't count.

Your data

Two small files, on your computer

Everything lives in a folder you choose (by default ~/good-habits). No cloud, no account, no health data. Open them with any text editor, delete them whenever you like.

If your assistant can't save files (for example claude.ai), nothing is stored and numbers stay approximate.

habits.json

Your habits and targets: "water, 1500 ml a day", "in bed by 23:00".

log.jsonl

What you did, day by day. Journal habits record only that you wrote, never what.

Reminders

Gentle nudges, if your assistant can

The skill checks what your assistant can do and tells you in one line. Nothing is lost when moving up.

You start the chat

No reminders: write when you like. Help choosing habits and check-ins in conversation, no exact counts.

Your calendar reminds you

Exact records in your folder, plus an .ics calendar file for habits with a fixed time.

A daily check-in

One check-in a day that knows where you are ("1000 of 1500 ml today") and a summary on Sunday. Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT.
